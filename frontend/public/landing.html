Diagnostic Bottleneck

Rare-disease diagnosis is a knowledge retrieval problem.

Over 7,000 recognized rare conditions often take years to pinpoint. Clinicians navigate incomplete phenotyping, siloed datasets, and population biases that obscure rare variants.

clinical_notes
CHALLENGE 01

Clinical Complexity

Clinical cases contain scattered phenotype observations, inconsistent non-standardized terminology, and fragmented symptom chronologies that evade generic LLM parsers.

Unstructured Electronic Health Records
schema
CHALLENGE 02

Knowledge Fragmentation

Relevant clinical evidence is distributed across phenotype ontologies, genetic databases, disconnected case reports, and rapidly multiplying biomedical literature.

High Cross-Ontology Disconnect
public
CHALLENGE 03

Population Underrepresentation

Reference resources are weighted towards some ancestries, so allele frequencies can be unrepresentative for others. This project reports South Asian (gnomAD) frequencies next to global ones; Indian-specific data (IndiGen) are not available to it.

Global Reference Baseline Skew

Demographic Genome Variation

Population-aware rare-disease prioritization.

Global genomic frequency data may not fully represent the rich genetic variation observed across Indian populations. The platform reports population-frequency evidence next to phenotype and retrieval evidence, and keeps it separate from the ranking score.

Evidence currently comes from gnomAD v4.1 (global and South Asian) joined to ClinVar pathogenic variants at gene level. IndiGen data are not available, gnomAD South Asian is not an India-specific dataset, and the population weight is 0, so no re-weighting is applied and no benefit is claimed.
